# No Barrier

## Why it matters

No Barrier is an AI medical interpretation startup aimed at turning language access into a reliable clinical infrastructure layer.

## What it does

- Real-time medical interpretation for hospitals and clinics
- HIPAA-aligned workflow
- Human-in-the-loop safeguards
- Designed for clinical encounters, not generic translation

## Why this is interesting

This is a strong healthcare AI wedge because the buyer is clear, the pain is real, and the workflow is tightly operational:

- LEP communication slows visits
- Human interpreters are costly and capacity-limited
- Language access is a compliance requirement, not a nice-to-have
- The ROI can be framed in time saved, cost reduced, and quality improved

## Watchpoints

- Can it maintain accuracy and latency in live care settings?
- Does human oversight stay scalable?
- Can the product expand from interpretation into broader language ops?
